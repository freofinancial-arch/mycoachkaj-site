Texting Terms

Last updated October 30, 2026

The program

My Coach Kaj is a text message coaching program for women on, or coming off, a GLP-1 medication. Kaj is an AI coach. Kaj texts you encouragement, check ins, and replies to the messages you send.

Texts are sent by My Coach Kaj, a business name of Next 10 Capital LLC. Questions go to support@mycoachkaj.com.

These terms cover our texts. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information. Read the Privacy Policy.

Joining and frequency

You join by checking the text consent box on our survey and entering your mobile number.

After you join, Kaj sends you a welcome text. It confirms you are enrolled and tells you how to stop.

Message frequency varies. Most of Kaj's texts are replies to messages you send. Kaj may also check in with you on her own, for example in the morning or after a quiet stretch.

Kaj texts are coaching messages. We do not use this number to send you sales offers. Price changes, surveys, and offers come by email from My Coach Kaj.

STOP, HELP, and START

  • To stop texts, reply STOP at any time. You can also reply END, QUIT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, or STOPALL. You will get one final text confirming you are unsubscribed. After that, Kaj sends nothing.
  • For help, reply HELP, or email support@mycoachkaj.com.
  • To restart, reply START after stopping.

Stopping texts does not cancel your billing. To cancel billing, go to mycoachkaj.com/billing, or write to support. When you text STOP or CANCEL, you get one text with that billing link.

Your price

Insiders keep the price they joined at for as long as their coaching continues. If your coaching ends and you return later, you pay the current price. Kaj keeps your history, so you pick up where you left off.

Costs, carriers, and your information

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile plan decides what texting costs you. My Coach Kaj does not charge per text.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We never share your mobile number or your consent to receive texts with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Our Privacy Policy explains everything else we do with your information.

Important limits

  • Adults only. You must be 18 or older to join.
  • Kaj is not medical care. Kaj is a habits coach, not a doctor, therapist, or emergency service. Questions about your medication or dose belong with your prescriber.
  • In an emergency, call 911. For crisis support in the US, call or text 988. If a message suggests you may be in crisis, Kaj sends you crisis resources and our team is alerted. Our team is not available around the clock.
  • Your phone, your thread. If you change your number, tell us at support@mycoachkaj.com so Kaj does not text someone else.
  • Changes. We may update these terms. If a change affects you, we will email you first.
