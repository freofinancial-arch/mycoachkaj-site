How Kaj works

How do you say Kaj?

Think sky, minus the s. Say it however you like, though. Kaj answers either way.

Why it works

Why texting, when apps never stuck?

You already read every text you get. There is no app to open, no streak to keep, and nothing to log. Kaj meets you in the thread you check fifty times a day, right in the moment the old habit shows up. The hard moments happen in the kitchen, at lunch, and on the couch at night. That is where Kaj is.

What is Kaj’s coaching based on?

Kaj draws on well studied coaching methods. Motivational interviewing helps you find your own reasons to change instead of being told. Acceptance and commitment therapy helps you notice a hard thought without obeying it. Behavioral activation builds change through small actions you can take today. Habit research shapes how Kaj helps new routines last.

You will not hear any of those names in your texts. You will just notice that Kaj asks good questions and never lectures.

How does Kaj decide what to say to me?

Kaj starts with what you share when you join: where you are with your medication, what feels hardest, what you want to feel better about, and how you like help on a hard day. Then Kaj listens. Every text you send teaches Kaj your words, your patterns, and what actually helps you.

Kaj also pays attention to timing, like the time of day and where you are in your shot week. So the message you get on a rough side effect day is not the one you get on a good Saturday.

How does Kaj get to know me?

The same way a good coach does. By remembering. Kaj remembers what you said last week, the dinner you were nervous about, and the win you almost brushed off. You never have to start over or repeat your story. The more you text, the more Kaj knows you.

How is this different from a diet program?

There is no meal plan, no points, and no food tracking. Your medication is already changing your body. Kaj works on everything it cannot touch: the guilt voice around food, habits that hold when life gets busy, and getting used to the woman in the mirror.

What happens when I stop my medication?

This is one of the most important times Kaj is built for. Many women fear the weight will come back when the medication ends. Kaj helps you get ready before that day comes, and stays with you after it. You will not be starting over. You are not the same woman who started.

The basics

Is Kaj a person?

No. Kaj is AI, and she will always tell you so.

Does Kaj replace my doctor?

No. Kaj coaches the daily habits and the thinking around your journey. Kaj never advises on your dose, timing, or switching or stopping your medication. Those questions always go to your prescriber. If you are ever in crisis, call or text 988.

Insiders join by invitation at $27 a month. Standard membership is $47 a month. Joining the Insider list is free, and you pay nothing until you accept your invitation. Your invitation spells out exactly how your rate works before you pay anything.

Go to mycoachkaj.com/billing. It takes about a minute.

How often will Kaj text me?

Kaj checks in at the time of day you choose. In your first two weeks, Kaj reaches out a little more while you get to know each other. After that, it is about once a day or less, and less when you are already texting. Kaj always answers when you text, at any hour.

Who sees what I share?

Your words are used to coach you and to make Kaj better. Our small team may read conversations to keep Kaj safe and helpful, with names hidden wherever possible. We never sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.
